GOOD QUESTIONS

Clarity starts here.

Is a Manduva house suitable for a modern family?

Yes, when thoughtfully planned. Traditional courtyards and verandahs can be combined with modern kitchens, attached bathrooms, contemporary lighting, accessibility and building services.

Can SpaceBox design 2, 3, 4 or 5-bedroom Manduva homes?

These configurations are part of our proposed design offering. Their feasibility depends on plot size, setbacks, permissions, budget and structural design.

Can I use a Manduva home for functions or rental income?

A home may be planned for hosted stays or intimate celebrations where permitted. Revenue is not guaranteed; local permissions, access, parking, neighbourhood conditions and operations must be assessed first.

Can the design follow Vastu?

We include your Vastu preferences early in the brief and coordinate with your preferred consultant, while retaining responsibility for safety, approvals, accessibility and practical design.
