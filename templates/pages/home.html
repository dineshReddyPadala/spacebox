Too many moving parts.

  • Separate designer, suppliers and contractors
  • Scope and material assumptions may differ
  • Changes can be difficult to trace

THE SPACEBOX APPROACH

Designed and coordinated together.

  • One coordinated design-to-handover brief
  • Documented specifications and BOQ
  • Agreed review and approval checkpoints
  • Managed procurement, quality and handover
WELLNESS BY DESIGN

A healthier space is a better kind of luxury.

Good design considers the life happening inside: sleep and privacy, air and light, movement and safety, acoustics and comfort.

  • Lighting and visual comfort
  • Healthy material and ventilation considerations
  • Sleep-first rooms and ergonomic workplaces
  • Senior-friendly and accessible interiors
  • Clear, practical recommendations—not unsupported medical promises
