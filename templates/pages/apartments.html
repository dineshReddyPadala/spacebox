GOOD QUESTIONS

Clarity starts here.

Will I see a design before execution?

Yes. The proposed experience includes a requirements brief, space planning, design options and approval before approved fabrication and installation begin.

How will the project cost be explained?

The indicative estimator is a planning guide only. A detailed quotation should be based on measured quantities, specifications, inclusions, exclusions and an approved scope.

Can SpaceBox include Vastu or wellness requirements?

Yes. We can consider family Vastu preferences and practical healthy-building design principles. These do not replace engineering checks, regulated approvals or medical advice.

How are changes managed?

After design freeze, requested changes should be documented, priced and approved before execution so cost and timing implications are clear.

Do you offer warranties?

Warranty terms should be defined project-by-project for each product and service. The prototype does not promise a fixed warranty duration before official company policies are confirmed.
